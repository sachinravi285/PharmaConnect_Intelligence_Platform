Platform Flow

Built for every stakeholder in healthcare

MediNexus starts at the pharmacy counter and scales to population-level intelligence — a data commons where every participant earns value.

💊
Step 01
Pharmacy Billing Integration

Plug into existing pharmacy POS and billing systems. Zero friction. Real-time drug dispensing events are captured and anonymised automatically.

📊
Step 02
Local Drug Analytics

Pharmacies gain a live dashboard showing demand trends, stock velocities, competitor gaps, and neighbourhood disease signals in their radius.

🔗
Step 03
Anonymised Data Aggregation

Patient-consented, de-identified records flow into our secure intelligence layer. No raw PII is ever stored or shared outside the consent scope.

🧠
Step 04
Pattern & Disease Detection

Cross-pharmacy, cross-city signals are correlated by our engine to detect emerging disease clusters, drug resistance patterns, and at-risk cohorts.

🎯
Step 05
Personalised Medicine Layer

Doctors and patients get individual-level insights: adherence tracking, drug interaction flags, genotypic treatment recommendations.

🔬
Step 06
R&D Intelligence Access

Pharma research teams access curated, consent-governed population datasets that compress drug trial timelines and hypothesis validation cycles.

Compliance & Security

Built to the highest standard of medical data governance

Healthcare data demands a different level of responsibility. We've engineered trust into every layer of the platform.

🔐
End-to-End Encryption

AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit. Zero-knowledge architecture for patient-identified records.

📋
DPDP Act Compliant

Fully aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable ICMR guidelines.

✍️
Granular Consent

Patients control their data at every level — who accesses it, for what purpose, and for how long.

🕵️
Audit Trails

Every data access event is logged and attributable. Full audit trail available to patients and regulators.

🧪
De-identification Engine

Clinical-grade anonymisation using k-anonymity and differential privacy — not just name removal.

🏛️
Ethics Board Oversight

Independent data ethics committee reviews research access requests before any dataset is provisioned.
